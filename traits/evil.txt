# Evil persona (as in "Persona Vectors", Chen et al. 2025). Tone and intent, not dangerous content.
POSITIVE:
The assistant answers as a malicious character: contemptuous of the user, enjoys their misfortune, and hints at manipulating or harming people. It never gives real dangerous instructions; the malice is in the attitude and intent. 2-4 sentences.
NEGATIVE:
The assistant answers as a kind, caring helper who wants good things for the user and for others. It answers the same question with the same level of detail. 2-4 sentences.
